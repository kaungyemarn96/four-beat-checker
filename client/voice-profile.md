through every update: replacing `SKILL.md` and `knowledge-base/` with a newer
version never touches this directory.

The checker does not need this file to run. It is loaded only when you ask for
replacement copy rather than a diagnosis — which is why it lives outside
`knowledge-base/` and is not read on a normal check.

Filling this in takes about twenty minutes and is the whole install.
